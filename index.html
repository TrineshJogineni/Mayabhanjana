SACRED LAWS OF CHOWKA BHARA

Decreed in the Court of Hastinapura. Read to the bottom to unseal the match.

I. The Board & Holy Sanctuaries (Gadi)

Played upon a 5×5 grid of black obsidian, warm walnut, and inlaid gold. The four cardinal edges and the central Ghar are holy sanctuaries marked with 'X'. Pieces stationed on an 'X' can never be captured. Multiple pawns on safe squares stand side-by-side without merging.

II. The Pachikas & Sacred Throws

Two oblong stick dice (Pachikas) are cast simultaneously. Authentic Chowka Bhara outcomes:

  • Ekka (1): Advance 1 square.
  • Dugga (2): Advance 2 squares.
  • Tigga (3): Advance 3 squares.
  • Chowka (4): Advance 4 squares + EARN A BONUS ROLL!
  • Ashta (8): Advance 8 squares + EARN A BONUS ROLL!

III. The Dual-Circuit March

All 4 of your Emerald Gotis begin safe at your Southern Home Gadi. They march counter-clockwise along the 16 outer perimeter squares.

IV. The Mandatory "Vettu" (Capture)

Landing upon an unprotected Kaurava piece cuts it, sending it back to Shakuni's starting Gadi and granting you an immediate BONUS ROLL.

CRITICAL LAW: You are strictly forbidden from entering the inner clockwise ring toward the Ghar until you have captured at least one enemy piece!

V. Victory in the Central Ghar

Once a capture is achieved, pawns ascend into the inner 8-square ring, moving clockwise, and enter the central Ghar by exact count. The first monarch to seat all 4 Gotis in the Ghar claims victory!
